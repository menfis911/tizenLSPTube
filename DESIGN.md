# LSPTube design

Operate surface: a brief TV launcher, remote-operated at viewing distance.
Approved identity: LSPTube. Native vector mark combines L and projection/play geometry.
Background #10292f; foreground #f4faf8; accent #62e2ce; error #ffc2b5.
Use one wordmark and one status sentence. Details are available on failure, not as a wall of implementation text during normal startup. Focus ring 4px solid #62e2ce. Body 30px at 1080p, title 72px, system sans for this utility screen. No decorative animation; status is text, not color alone.
Icons: assets/icon.svg is editable master, scripts/render-icons.swift reproduces PNGs on macOS.
