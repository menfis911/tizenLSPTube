# LSPTube design

Operate surface: a brief TV launcher, remote-operated at viewing distance.
Identity: LSPTube. The user preferred the replacement lens mark: ivory ring, amber crescent and highlight.
Background #182128; foreground #f4f0e6; accent #ffb34d; error #ffc2b5.
Use one wordmark and one status sentence. Details are available on failure, not as a wall of implementation text during normal startup. Focus ring 4px solid amber. Body 30px at 1080p, title 72px, self-hosted PT Sans with Cyrillic (OFL-1.1). No decorative animation; status is text, not color alone. Preserve TV-native navigation and place translation in playback settings.
Icons: assets/icon.svg is editable master, scripts/render-icons.swift reproduces PNGs on macOS.
