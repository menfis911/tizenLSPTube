Player uses projector IP 192.168.100.95; changing DHCP IP requires rebuilding this test.
Start and Player replace the earlier launcher and TizenTube respectively. Partner signing required.
Original service binds proxy to LAN: use on a trusted home network. TLS verification is retained.
Playback and service survival when switching applications remain unverified on hardware. Casting/DIAL is untested. No Yandex translation yet.
