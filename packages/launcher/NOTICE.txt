Restores official Cobalt native metadata, proxy 127.0.0.2:8101 and original service autostart behavior.

Same application/package identity: replaces the existing TizenTube test app, retaining its data where supported.
Unsigned package: sign through Apps2Samsung Partner Signing. No account credentials are included.
After installation cold reboot the projector, wait 20 seconds, then open TizenTube Test Cobalt.
Expected: native TV interface without pressing the former web-proxy button.
Hardware playback, script injection, remote control and sign-in are unverified. No Yandex translation is included yet.
